First chess steps for little ones

Baby Chess turns chess into tiny, happy games for children aged about 3 to 6. Each level teaches one friendly piece in about a minute.

Coming soon to Google Play

Made for little hands

Big pieces, tap-tap moves and glowing dots that show where a piece can go. No reading needed: everything is spoken.

A friendly opponent

Bandu Monkey plays like a beginner, so children win most games. When it does win, it just dances.

Stickers to collect

Every finished level earns a sticker for the sticker book. There's no "game over" and no timers.

Safe by design

No ads, no tracking, no accounts. It works offline. Grown-up settings are behind a press-and-hold button.
