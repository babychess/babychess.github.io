Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · applies to the Baby Chess app for Android

In short: Baby Chess does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

Who we are

Baby Chess is a chess-learning game for young children, made by an independent developer. You can reach us at babychessgame@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Baby Chess does not ask for, collect or send us any personal information, from children or adults. We don't use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party services that collect data.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Game progress and settings: which levels have been played, stars, stickers and grown-up settings such as voice speed.
  • Optional voice recordings: a grown-up can record their own voice for the spoken prompts in the grown-up settings. The app asks for microphone permission only when the grown-up first taps record, and the microphone is used only while they record. Recordings are saved only on the device and are never uploaded or shared.

This information never leaves the device through Baby Chess. We cannot see it.

Backups by your device

Android may include the app's saved progress in your own device backup (Android Auto Backup) to your Google account, so progress can be restored if you reinstall. That backup is run by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. You can turn backups off in your device settings.

Spoken prompts

The game's spoken instructions use your device's built-in text-to-speech service. Only the game's own text, such as "Your turn!", is spoken. If your device's speech service uses an online voice, that text is handled by your speech provider under its own privacy policy. No personal information is involved.

Internet and permissions

  • Internet: the app works fully offline. We do not send data over the internet. The permission is part of the standard app framework.
  • Microphone: optional, used only for the grown-up voice recording described above.

Children's privacy

Baby Chess is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because we collect no personal information, no data about children is collected, used or disclosed. There are no ads, no chat, no links to social media, and no way for a child to share information. Grown-up settings are behind a press-and-hold button. Any future purchases will be behind a check that young children can't pass.

Purchases

Baby Chess currently has no purchases. If optional in-app purchases are added later, payment will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will never see payment details. We will update this policy before any such change takes effect.

Deleting data

Everything is stored on your device. To delete it, use Reset stickers & progress in the grown-up settings, delete recordings in Record my own voice, or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email babychessgame@gmail.com.
